Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Claude Trading Alerts is a personal, non-promotional stock and options research notification service operated by its registered sole proprietor.

Information We Collect

When SMS enrollment becomes available, we may collect your mobile phone number, SMS consent status, and subscription preferences.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to administer subscriptions and send requested trading research and system notifications.

SMS Communications

SMS messages may include market observations, options research updates, significant price movements, and system notifications. Message frequency varies.

Information Sharing

We use messaging service providers, including Twilio, to transmit SMS notifications. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Opting Out

Recipients can reply STOP to stop SMS notifications or HELP for assistance.

Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect subscription information and limit access to authorized users.

Changes

This policy may be updated if our notification practices change.
